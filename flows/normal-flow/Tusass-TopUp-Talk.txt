
1. Navigate to `${COS_URL}`

2. Click the Escape button to show the **Login with password** button.

3. Click the **Login with password** button.

4. Enter Username in the input box: `${ADMIN_USER}`

5. Enter Password in the input box: `${ADMIN_PASS}`

6. Click the **Log in** button.

7. In the Search box, enter `${MISTIN_ID}`

8. After the search result is shown, take a screenshot of the page and save it as `search-result.png`
   **Screenshot 1**

9. Note the current balance on the page and save it in the variable `current_balance`.

10. Navigate to `${PORTAL_URL}`

11. Click the **TopUp Talk** button.

12. On the TopUp Talk page, enter your phone number `${PHONE_NUMBER}` in the input box.

13. On the same page, enter your mobile number `${MOBILE_NUMBER}` in the input box.

14. Click the **Continue** button.

15. Choose the `${TOPUP_AMOUNT}` top-up amount and click the **Continue** button.

16. Click the **Continue** button again.

17. On the payment page, enter the card details:

* Card Number: `${CARD_NUMBER}`
* Expiry Date: `${CARD_EXPIRY}`
* CVC: `${CARD_CVC}`

18. Click the **Pay** button.

19. After the payment is successful, the Receipt page will be displayed.
    Take a screenshot of the page and save it as `receipt.png`
    **Screenshot 2**

20. Navigate to `${COS_URL}`

21. Verify that the current balance is updated with the top-up amount.
    Take a screenshot of the updated balance page and save it as `updated-balance.png`
    **Screenshot 3**

22. Verify the balance update by comparing:

* Previous balance: `current_balance`
* Top-up amount: `${TOPUP_AMOUNT}`

23. Generate the PDF report with the following message:

`Tusass TopUp Talk flow is executed successfully. ${MISTIN_ID} is topped up with amount ${TOPUP_AMOUNT} and the new balance is ${NEW_BALANCE}.`
